> Delicate hand-painted watercolor illustration, soft wet-on-wet washes, subtle cold-press paper grain, romantic and luxurious.
> Palette strictly: burgundy velvet #4d1225 with shadows #2d0915, wine #802a44, peony pink #ad5078, blush #f1c2d6,
> lilac #b9a0c9, ivory #fbf6f1, tiny touches of candle gold #d9a75a. Peonies have golden-ochre stamens; leaves are muted taupe-sage
